Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Progress tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel weekly access?

On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Settled. On Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the week ends to avoid the next charge.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There’s no account, so progress doesn’t move between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress tab › Clear all progress.

Are these the real test questions?

No. All practice questions and explanations are our own. They are not the real test questions.
