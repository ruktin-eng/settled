ASPIRING_SOLICITOR.DOC
Commercial law · Life sciences

From the pharmacy to commercial law

My interest in law started while studying pharmacy. Cases like the UK Supreme Court’s 2018 decision invalidating Pfizer’s second medical use patent for pregabalin, and laws like the Human Medicines Regulations 2012, showed me how much law shapes how medicines are developed, sold and regulated.

I want to become a commercial solicitor in the life sciences sector, working on the patents, regulation and deals that bring new treatments to patients. My clinical experience means I understand what those products mean for the people who use them.
